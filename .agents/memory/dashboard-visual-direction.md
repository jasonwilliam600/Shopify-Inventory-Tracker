---
name: Dashboard visual direction
description: The preferred Top Speed Hobbies dashboard theme and rejected visual alternative.
---

Use the owner's approved TSH Inventory Sync branding showcase as the visual
direction: blue/orange gear-and-package branding, compact navigation, and
coordinated light/dark surfaces. This supersedes the earlier instruction to
preserve the refined dashboard layout.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved a whole-application redesign matching the
uploaded TSH Inventory Sync mockup. The earlier racing-themed Velocity alternative
was rejected; that rejection does not prohibit the newly approved TSH branding.

**How to apply:** Follow the approved TSH showcase across all existing routes.
Screenshot metrics and account names are examples, not factual application data;
never copy them into live views or imply unverified supplier health.

For the Dashboard, use the later approved seven-card correction: three supplier
network shortcuts, then four separate Shopify-store shortcuts, followed by a
full-width supplier table. All summary cards must be functional navigation,
not decorative metrics.

**Why:** The owner explicitly identified functional card destinations as the key
requirement of the dashboard correction.

**How to apply:** Route to real, matching filtered data or an honest unavailable
explanation. Never substitute supplier inventory for Shopify stock, fabricate
historical trend percentages, or label an import timestamp as verified health.

Keep the store connection badge at the top of the dashboard, even when connection details are also shown below.

**Why:** The user said the badge is "very important info and needs to be at the top."

**How to apply:** Do not remove the top badge as redundant when simplifying connection information.

The primary UI font for the entire TSH Inventory Sync application is
**Inter**, including customizable authentication UI. Do not modify the TSH logo
artwork or its custom lettering.

**Why:** The owner explicitly replaced the earlier Space Grotesk selection with
Inter to match the cleaner approved dashboard reference, while preserving logo
artwork and application behavior.

**How to apply:** Use 400 for body/table content, 500 for secondary information,
600 for navigation/buttons/card labels, 700 for headings, and 800 for large
dashboard statistics. Keep sizes, line heights and tracking clean rather than
heavy or cramped. Typography changes must not redesign the seven-card dashboard.

Support Light, Dark and System appearance with the approved TSH branding and
Inter typography.

**Why:** The owner requested application-wide theme consistency, including
customizable authentication UI, with a remembered selection and OS-following mode.

**How to apply:** Future screens and dialogs must inherit the shared appearance;
do not introduce light-only surfaces or a separate authentication theme.

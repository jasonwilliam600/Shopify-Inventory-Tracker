---
name: Dashboard visual direction
description: The preferred Top Speed Hobbies dashboard theme and rejected visual alternative.
---

Use the refined dashboard's calm visual direction as the basis for further dashboard work. Do not switch to the racing-themed Velocity direction without a new explicit request.

**Why:** The user rejected the racing variant: "that looks horrible. revert back to the other theme", then continued dashboard work from the previous refined theme.

**How to apply:** Preserve the preferred theme when adding cards or store information. The presence of the racing variant in the canvas or source does not imply approval.

Keep the store connection badge at the top of the dashboard, even when connection details are also shown below.

**Why:** The user said the badge is "very important info and needs to be at the top."

**How to apply:** Do not remove the top badge as redundant when simplifying connection information.

The final primary font for the entire TSH Inventory Sync application is
**Space Grotesk**, including customizable authentication UI.

**Why:** The owner explicitly selected it as the final font and requested a bold,
modern technology-platform appearance without changing the blue-and-white theme,
layout, functionality or business logic.

**How to apply:** Use 700 for major page titles and section headings, 600 for
navigation/buttons/labels, and 400 or 500 for body text/tables through shared
typography settings. Remove decorative/serif and italicized display treatments.
The owner specified numeric weight 700 for titles even when calling it ExtraBold;
do not substitute 800 or invent a different font.

Support Light, Dark and System appearance without changing the existing branding,
Space Grotesk typography or page layouts.

**Why:** The owner requested application-wide theme consistency, including
customizable authentication UI, with a remembered selection and OS-following mode.

**How to apply:** Future screens and dialogs must inherit the shared appearance;
do not introduce light-only surfaces or a separate authentication theme.

## 1. Saved Proposals — Interactive Management

Currently, saved proposals appear as static rows without management controls.

Make each proposal row clickable.

When clicked, open an expandable detail panel or modal matching the existing dark-theme design.

Display all available information:

- Supplier name
- Original supplier SKU
- Supplier product title
- Proposed historical catalog SKU
- Historical catalog product title
- Selected correction reason
- Verification confirmations
- Proposal status
- Date and time created
- Reviewer identity, when available

Do not fabricate missing information.

### Required actions

**View Details**
- Clicking anywhere on a proposal row opens its details.
- Support mouse and keyboard interaction.
- Allow closing the details and returning to the same list position.

**Edit Proposal**
- Allow editing inactive proposals.
- Permit changing the historical catalog destination and correction reason.
- Reuse the existing searchable historical catalog selector.
- Require renewed verification if the destination changes.
- Preserve the original proposal identity and modification history.
- Validate changes server-side.
- Do not create a duplicate proposal when editing.

**Delete Proposal**
- Provide a clearly labeled Delete Proposal button.
- Require confirmation before deletion.
- Include the supplier SKU and proposed destination in the confirmation.
- Provide Cancel and Confirm Delete options.
- Remove deleted proposals from the active list after successful deletion.
- Preserve an audit record of deletion without retaining the proposal as active.
- Never delete supplier inventory records, existing approved mappings, manufacturer rules, or protected holds.

**Duplicate Prevention**
- Detect existing inactive proposals with the same supplier, original SKU, and proposed destination.
- Prevent accidental duplicate submissions.
- Offer to open the existing proposal instead.
- Handle duplicate checks server-side to prevent race conditions.
- Do not automatically delete existing duplicates or their audit records.

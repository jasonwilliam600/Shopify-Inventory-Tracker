### PART 2 — BUILD THE SKU MAPPING MANAGER

Add a section to the existing dashboard named **SKU Mapping Manager**.

This will be my control center for correcting and managing supplier-to-Shopify SKU relationships.

#### A. Main screen

Show a searchable, filterable table containing:

- Supplier name.
- Original supplier SKU.
- Suggested Shopify SKU.
- Supplier quantity.
- Last supplier inventory update.
- Mapping status.
- Block/rejection reason.
- Shopify product information when available.

Support Heartland, HobbyTyme, Walthers, and MMD/Squadron.

Provide filters for:

- Unmatched
- Candidate
- Flagged
- Rejected
- Ambiguous
- Approved

#### B. Fix Mapping

Add a **Fix Mapping** action for individual records.

When selected:

1. Display the supplier's original SKU and product information.
2. Display the currently proposed or previously rejected Shopify SKU.
3. Allow me to search the CURRENT Shopify product and variant catalog.
4. Display matching Shopify product titles, SKUs, vendor/manufacturer, variant options, and identifying information.
5. Allow me to select the correct Shopify variant.
6. Warn about duplicate SKUs, incompatible products, selling-unit differences, and existing conflicting relationships.
7. Require explicit confirmation before approval.
8. Store the approved relationship permanently using the Shopify variant ID—not just a text SKU.

Use historical Shopify data only for preview. Never approve a historical-only candidate as a verified live relationship.

#### C. Correcting previously rejected SKUs

I must be able to correct any of the 12 rejected mappings or other held relationships.

Provide a controlled **Resolve & Approve** process that:

- Requires explicit confirmation of the correct product and retail selling unit.
- Preserves the original rejected relationship and its reason in an audit history.
- Supersedes only the specific hold being resolved.
- Does not remove unrelated safety blocks.
- Prevents legacy prefix or exact-item rules from overriding an active hold.

A corrected SKU must not remain permanently blocked after a properly authorized resolution.

#### D. Permanent translation

Use:

**Supplier ID + Original Supplier SKU → Approved Shopify Variant ID**

Requirements:

- Original supplier SKU must be preserved.
- Multiple wholesalers may map to one Shopify variant.
- Same-supplier duplicate mappings must trigger collision review.
- Never automatically add quantities from potentially overlapping supplier offers.
- Approved mappings must survive supplier inventory updates.
- Changes to Shopify product identity must trigger review rather than silently moving mappings.
- SKU-and-quantity-only supplier updates must remain supported.
- Do not require UPCs, titles, descriptions, or prices in every inventory update.

#### E. Bulk mapping review

Add the ability to review and approve multiple mapping candidates efficiently.

Show each supplier/Shopify relationship and associated warnings before confirmation.

Never bulk-approve rejected mappings, product-identity conflicts, duplicate Shopify SKU ambiguities, or selling-unit conflicts without their required individual verification.

Keep an audit trail of mapping approvals and corrections.
